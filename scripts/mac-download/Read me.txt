Install Sidemates

1. Double-click "Install Sidemates".
2. If macOS says it can't check it for malicious software, click Done. Then open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to
   "Install Sidemates". Confirm with Open. macOS may ask for your Mac's password or
   Touch ID to allow this; Sidemates itself never asks for it.
3. A Terminal window shows the install, and your studio opens in the browser.

Pictures of each step: https://sidemates.app/mac-download/

Sidemates isn't signed by Apple yet, which is why macOS asks. The installer checks
the bundle's fingerprint before using it. Remove Sidemates any time with:
"~/Library/Application Support/Sidemates/uninstall.sh"
