Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. OpenBot is free, open-source software. Your teammates use an AI you connect — OpenBot never charges anything on top.

Does my data leave my Mac?

Your team, chats and files live on your Mac. When a teammate works, what it needs goes to the AI you chose. Pick a model on your Mac to keep everything local.

What do they do without asking?

Read, search, write and make files in their own workspace. Anything that sends, buys, signs in or submits waits for you.

Does it work when my Mac sleeps?

They work while your Mac is on, and OpenBot keeps it awake when a scheduled job is due. It can also run on an always-on computer.

Can I use it from my iPhone?

Yes. Pair your iPhone with one scan to chat, get notified and approve from your Home Screen.

Why Terminal?

It lets us ship a free Mac app without a paid Apple certificate. It downloads from our public GitHub releases, checks the fingerprint and never asks for your password.
