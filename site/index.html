What the installer does
  • Downloads OpenBot for your Mac and checks its fingerprint before using it.
  • Installs it in ~/Library/Application Support/OpenBot — no administrator password.
  • Runs it quietly in the background and starts it when you log in.
  • Adds OpenBot to Applications and your Dock, then opens your studio.

To remove it: "~/Library/Application Support/OpenBot/uninstall.sh" (your data stays unless you add --delete-data).

Requires macOS 13 or later on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.
