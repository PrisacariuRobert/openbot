Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. OpenBot is free, open-source software. Your team uses an AI you connect, and OpenBot never charges anything on top.

Does my stuff leave my Mac?

Your team, chats and files live on your Mac. When a teammate works, what it needs goes to the AI you picked. Choose a model on your Mac to keep everything local.

What do they do without asking?

Search, read, write and make files in their own workspace. Anything that sends, books, buys, signs in or submits waits for you.

Does it work while my Mac sleeps?

They work while your Mac is on, and OpenBot keeps it awake when a scheduled job is due. It can also run on a computer that’s always on.

Can I use it on my iPhone?

Yes. Pair your iPhone with one scan to chat, get notified and approve things from your Home Screen.

Why Terminal?

It lets us give you a free Mac app without a paid Apple certificate. It downloads from our public GitHub releases, checks the fingerprint and never asks for your password.
