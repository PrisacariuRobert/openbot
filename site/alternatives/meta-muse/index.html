Sidemates and Meta Muse compared

Meta Muse is a personal agent that lives in Meta's cloud and now has a Mac app. Sidemates is an open-source team that runs on your Mac, with the AI you choose.

Last updated 7 October 2026. Details about Meta Muse come from its public pages (linked below) and can change. If something is out of date, tell us. See the full comparison with the other options.

What Meta Muse is

Meta launched Muse in the US on 8 September 2026. It is a personal agent that runs in its own secure virtual machine in Meta's cloud, with its own browser, on Meta's Muse Spark model. It sends emails, books travel and plans larger goals, checks with you before sensitive actions such as sending an email or making a purchase, and keeps an audit trail. It works on iPhone, Android and the web, reached Canada on 18 September, and since 17 September has a Mac app that works in Mail, Messages, Calendar, Notes and files. It is free for most use, with paid plans reported at $20 and $100 a month. Meta says your conversations stay out of its ad systems, and you can opt out of your interactions being used to train its models.

When to choose which

Choose Meta Muse if you…

  • want a managed agent with nothing to set up, on your phone as well as your Mac
  • need it to keep working while your computer is off
  • are in the US or Canada and are happy for your agent and its data to live in Meta's cloud

Choose Sidemates if you…

  • want your data to stay on your Mac, or to use a model that runs there
  • want to choose the AI, or use one you already pay for
  • live outside the US and Canada
  • want a team of teammates, and free, open-source software you can read

Try Sidemates

On a Mac (macOS 13 or later), one line installs it. No administrator password, and the installer checks the download's fingerprint. Read the installer first if you like.

curl -fsSL https://sidemates.app/install.sh | sh

Then connect an AI (a free Gemini key is enough to start) and make your first teammate, or add a ready-made one from the gallery. Source code and docs: github.com/PrisacariuRobert/sidemates.

Questions

Is Sidemates an alternative to Meta Muse?

Yes, on a Mac. Both can work in your Mail, Messages, Calendar, Notes and files. Muse runs in Meta's cloud on Meta's own model and keeps working while your computer is off. Sidemates runs on your Mac with the AI you choose, and it is free, open-source software.

Is Meta Muse available outside the US?

As of 7 October 2026, Muse is available in the US and Canada. Sidemates runs on your own Mac, so it works wherever your Mac does.

Does Sidemates train AI on my data?

No. Sidemates has no servers and collects nothing. When a teammate works, what it needs goes to the AI provider you chose, under that provider's terms, and some providers may train on prompts, so check them. Choose a model on your Mac to keep everything local.
