sidemates One-line install

Install Sidemates without Terminal.

Download the disk image for your Mac and open it. It installs exactly what the one-line install does: same app, same background service, same studio.

Download for Apple silicon Download for Intel

Not sure which? Apple menu → About This Mac: “Chip: Apple M…” is Apple silicon; “Processor: … Intel” is Intel. About 140 MB. macOS 13 or later.

The disk image arrives with the next release of Sidemates. Until then, use the one-line install.

  1. Open “Install Sidemates”

    Open the downloaded disk image and double-click Install Sidemates. macOS says it can't check it. Click Done, not Move to Trash.

  2. Click “Open Anyway”

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Open Anyway next to “Install Sidemates”. macOS may ask for your Mac's password or Touch ID here; that's macOS asking, not Sidemates.

  3. Click “Open”, then wait

    The warning appears once more: click Open. A Terminal window shows the install, and your studio opens in the browser in a few minutes. You can eject the disk afterwards.

These are illustrations of macOS's own windows; the exact words can differ between macOS versions. Apple describes the same steps in Safely open apps on your Mac (published 27 May 2026, checked 8 October 2026).

Why macOS asks: Sidemates isn't signed by Apple yet. The installer checks the bundle's fingerprint before using it, needs no administrator password of its own, and leaves an uninstaller: "~/Library/Application Support/Sidemates/uninstall.sh"

Comfortable with Terminal? The one-line install does the same without these steps.
