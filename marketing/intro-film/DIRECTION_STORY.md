# From an idea to taken care of

## Why another cut

The second cut added camera movement but retained a repeated headline-plus-panel composition. Faster motion did not change the underlying feature-presentation structure. This version changes the editorial model: one launch request is the protagonist. Objects carry that request through collaboration, actual output, review and continuity.

## Direction

47 seconds, 60 fps. Black and white stage; the original vector teammates are the only colorful artwork. Use editorial-scale typography, purposeful close crops, clear depth and a single moving request. Avoid repeated orbit shots, card grids below headlines, decorative dissolves, constant camera drift, and fake performance statistics.

1. **0–6 / The ask.** Competing fragments of work contract into a single sentence. The sentence becomes the outgoing message. One decisive send, rather than a logo opener.
2. **6–14 / The team.** Follow the request through three teammates. Sources flow in, work is shared, one response returns. Animate responsibility, not decorative mascots.
3. **14–23 / The result.** Travel through a launch deck, web project and delivery plan as full-size editorial objects. They collect into one launch pack.
4. **23–30 / The decision.** Recurring work advances along a calendar, then stops at the human approval. Drop the score's percussion here. The user has the last move.
5. **30–38 / Continuity.** The approved conversation becomes the Mac and phone view. A passing message carries the eye between devices.
6. **38–47 / Yours.** A precise model-choice interaction, an extensibility/source-code reveal and the OpenBot signature. Keep the end frame calm and readable.

## Sound and production

Original locally synthesized, picture-specific score. Percussive first half, deliberate approval pause, warmer resolution. Crisp taps, paper movement and stereo travel follow meaningful actions. No paid generation, stock song, Apple assets, synthetic endorsement or invented customer proof. Existing cuts are retained.

The Creative Production board is not directly callable in this session; the editable Remotion project and rendered movie are the review surface. This is deterministic code-native motion, not a raster-generation workflow.

## Scope

Staged product narrative for creative review. Depicted sends, files, sites and cross-device state use fictional data. It is not a recording of a real successful workflow. Keep capability and readiness conditions in CAPABILITY_REVIEW.md, not visual badges. No publishing or release announcement is authorized by this revision.
