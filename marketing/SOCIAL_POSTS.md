## Replies and opt-in pilot conversations

Use these as guidance, not automated replies. Read what the person actually said.

- **“Is it free?”** The source is open. Model use can have provider fees or subscription limits. Explain the current supported setup; do not say unlimited/free forever.
- **“Is this just Grok Bot?”** Explain the similar category, then show the current source, provider choice and inspectable workflow. Do not imply affiliation or tested parity.
- **“Can I try it?”** Before release, explain the current setup and limits. Only invite them to a pilot after the critical fixes and safe installation have been checked.
- **“Why all the mascots?”** They are customizable teammate identities; the product still needs to earn its place through useful work.
- **A willing tester:** ask for one task, their Mac/OS and the result they expect. Do not ask for passwords, personal mail, tokens or a full private workspace dump.
- **A creator/community member who requested details:** send one tailored reply with the sample task and limitations. No scraped contact lists, unsolicited mass DMs or automated follow-up loops.

## Writing voice

Write as the person making the app: concrete, curious, willing to admit a limitation. Avoid “revolutionary,” “10×,” “the future of work,” “Grok killer,” “100% private,” “fully autonomous” and “all models” unless a narrowly defined claim is actually supported. Two relevant hashtags are enough if you use them at all; the demonstration matters more than a hashtag list.
